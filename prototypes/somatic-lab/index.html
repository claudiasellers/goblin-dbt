Somatic Lab

Nine small, clickable sketches of the Wave-1 body tools for Goblin DBT. Reference only: feel them, poke them, tell us what's off.

Best on your phone. Sensors (mic, motion) ask first and have touch fallbacks.
  1. 1
    Press and pushPress the phone between your palms, or push a wall, then feel the release.flooded · flooded + blank (step 1) · blank (fast) · far away
  2. 2
    Hand on heartA warm hand on your chest (or a mug, or your thighs), with slow soothing strokes.stirred · flooded · flooded + blank
  3. 3
    Squeeze and dropTighten a spot on purpose, then let go, and notice tight vs loose.stirred · flooded (release) · blank (tense)
  4. 4
    Voice roomHum to grow a moss creature, buzz like a hive, or siren up to wake things.stirred · flooded (hum) · blank (siren)
  5. 5
    Outside-in scanStart at your feet and the things you touch, then try a guess word: does this fit?steady · stirred · far away (contact points)
  6. 6
    Shake and stompShort bursts of shaking and stomping, then watch it settle.blank / heavy · stirred
  7. 7
    Pocket stoneFind something slightly okay, polish it into a stone, keep it, hold it later.steady · stirred (to make) · anyone (to hold)
  8. 8
    Sound compass + Edge glowFind soft chimes around you, then let your eyes widen to the screen's edges.everyone · safe default for flooded + blank and far away
  9. 9
    Yuck or queasy?Tell apart gross, sick and hungry, try a guess word, then push it away.steady · stirred
