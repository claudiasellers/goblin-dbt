Brain Visualization Styles

These are rough concept directions — imagine each one appearing after a check-in, with the relevant areas lit up. Click the one that feels right for Goblin DBT.

A. Abstract Network

Pure node-and-edge visualization. No brain shape — just the network. Glowing nodes sized by activation level. Feels like looking at a living system. Modern, techy, unique.

B. Anatomical Silhouette

Recognizable brain outline with regions glowing inside it. Feels educational, grounded, "here's where this is happening in your head." More medical/science feel.

C. Hybrid — Brain Shape + Network Overlay

Faint brain outline gives spatial context, but the real content is the glowing network overlaid on top. Nodes positioned anatomically. Network labels below. Best of both — grounded but not textbook.

D. Minimal / Constellation

Almost no visual structure — just glowing points in space, like a constellation. Text-forward: the pattern name and plain-language explanation do the heavy lifting. The viz is atmospheric, not informational. Subtle animation.
